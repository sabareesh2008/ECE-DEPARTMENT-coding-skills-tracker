# CodeMetrix Year I / Year II / Year III

Replace these five files in your current project:
- leetcode.html
- github.html
- script.js
- github-script.js
- style.css

Behavior:
- Both trackers open with three year cards.
- Year II is selected by default and all existing rows without a Year field are treated as Year II.
- Selecting a year filters the section cards, counts, section/overall leaderboard, champion calculation, search and leaderboard data to that year.
- If future data rows contain Year, Academic Year, Year Name, or year, values 1/2/3 (or I/II/III) are supported.
- Existing tracker HTML structure and functionality remain in place.
